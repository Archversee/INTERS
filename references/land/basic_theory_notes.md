## Parallel parking
Stop alongside the parked car in front of the space, about half a metre to a metre away
from it. Select reverse and check all round. Reverse slowly until the rear of your car is
level with the rear of the parked car, then steer fully to the left. When the car is at
about 45 degrees to the kerb, straighten the wheels. When the front of your car is clear of
the parked car, steer fully to the right to bring the front in. Straighten the wheels as
the car becomes parallel to the kerb, and stop.

## Observation when reversing
Check the mirrors and look over both shoulders before and during the manoeuvre. Stop if a
pedestrian or vehicle comes near.

## Final position
The car should end parallel to the kerb, close to it without touching, and within the
space. A short move forward to centre the car is acceptable.
