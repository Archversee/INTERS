## Airspeed limitations — wing flaps
Maximum flap extended speed (Vfe): flaps 10° 110 KIAS; flaps 10° to 30° 85 KIAS.
Do not extend flaps beyond 10° above 85 KIAS.

## Airspeed limitations
Never exceed speed (Vne) 163 KIAS.

## Normal takeoff
Wing flaps 0° to 10°. Carburettor heat cold. Throttle full open. Elevator control: lift
the nose wheel (rotate) at 55 KIAS. Climb speed 70 to 80 KIAS.

## Enroute climb
Normal climb airspeed 75 to 85 KIAS. Best rate of climb (Vy) at sea level 74 KIAS.
Throttle full open. Mixture full rich below 3000 ft.

## Before landing
Seats, belts secure. Fuel selector valve both. Mixture rich. Landing and taxi lights on as
required.

## Normal landing
Airspeed 65 to 75 KIAS with flaps up. Wing flaps as desired: below 110 KIAS for 10°,
below 85 KIAS for 10° to 30°. Airspeed 60 to 70 KIAS with flaps 30°. Touchdown on main
wheels first. Landing roll: lower the nose wheel gently. Braking minimum required.

## After landing
Wing flaps up. Carburettor heat cold. Landing light off when clear of the runway.

## Securing airplane
Parking brake set. Throttle idle. All electrical switches off. Mixture idle cut-off.
