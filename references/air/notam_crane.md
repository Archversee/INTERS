# NOTAM A0123/26 (sample)

> SAMPLE. A made-up NOTAM for the "new obstacle" demo scenario.

## Obstacle
Crane erected 1.2 nm west of the runway 03 centreline, abeam the threshold, 450 ft AGL,
lit. Valid until further notice.

## Circuit procedure
Left-hand circuits on runway 03: fly the downwind leg at least 1.5 nm from the runway
centreline to remain clear of the crane. Delay the descent so the base leg is flown clear
of the obstacle.
