## Taxiing
Release the parking brake and test the brakes as soon as the airplane starts moving. Taxi at
a speed that allows stopping or turning where and when desired, normally no faster than a
brisk walk in congested areas. Use the taxi light at night and in low visibility.

## Line-up and takeoff roll
Line up on the runway centreline. Turn on the strobe and landing light to be visible. Apply
full power smoothly and check engine instruments. Keep straight with rudder.

## Lift-off and initial climb
At rotation speed, apply back pressure to raise the nose to the lift-off attitude. Once
airborne, establish the climb attitude and accelerate to Vy. Maintain runway heading.

## Crosswind leg
The departure leg continues until beyond the departure end of the runway and within 300 ft
of traffic pattern altitude, then turn to the crosswind leg.

## Traffic pattern altitude
Traffic pattern altitude is usually 1,000 ft above the elevation of the airport surface.
Level off at pattern altitude and reduce power to maintain pattern airspeed.

## Downwind leg
Fly the downwind leg parallel to the landing runway at pattern altitude, at about one half to
one mile from the runway. Complete the before-landing checklist. Abeam the touchdown point,
reduce power and begin the descent; extend flaps as appropriate.

## Base leg
Turn base when the touchdown point is about 45° behind the wing. Extend further flaps and
establish the approach airspeed and descent rate.

## Final approach
Turn onto final to align with the runway centreline. Extend landing flaps. Stabilise the
approach airspeed and glide path; adjust power to control the descent rate.

## Round out and touchdown
Close the throttle and round out (flare) a few feet above the runway. Touch down on the main
wheels at minimum controllable airspeed.

## After-landing roll
Maintain directional control and slow to taxi speed before turning off the runway. Once
clear of the runway, complete the after-landing checklist: flaps up, unnecessary lights off.
